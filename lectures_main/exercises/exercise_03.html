02 · Build columns from geometry

Take three cross products

At each axis, let \({}^0p_{J_i}\) be a point on the axis and \({}^0\hat a_{J_i}\) its world direction. Both are evaluated at the current joint angles.

\[{}^0J_{P,i}=\begin{bmatrix}{}^0\hat a_{J_i}\times({}^0p_P-{}^0p_{J_i})\\{}^0\hat a_{J_i}\end{bmatrix},\qquad {}^0J_P=\begin{bmatrix}{}^0J_{P,1}&{}^0J_{P,2}&{}^0J_{P,3}\end{bmatrix}.\]

09 · Bonus · ABB CRB

Calculate \({}^3J_4\) for the six-joint tool twist

Use the ABB CRB 15000-5/0.95 URDF. Frame 0 is base_link, frame 3 is link_3, and \(O_4\) is the origin of link_4. The tool point is tool0, at the same position as the origin of link_6.

\[{}^3J_4\dot q=\begin{bmatrix}{}^3v_{6@4}\\{}^3\omega_6\end{bmatrix}\]

Here \({}^3v_{6@4}\) is the velocity of the tool’s instantaneous rigid motion evaluated at \(O_4\). Express that same six-joint tool twist along the axes of frame 3, with linear velocity first. Keep all six columns. This changes the reference point and the coordinate axes; do not subtract the motion of frame 3 or replace the task with the velocity of link 4 itself.
