What to change

Some visual origins contain quarter-turn rotations or flipped translation signs. Repair the visual xyz and rpy entries. Keep joint origins and axes unchanged.

Lengths are in metres; angles are in radians. Expressions such as pi/2 and -pi/2 are accepted.

Responses: 28 D–H parameters, 12 fixed-transform values, 48 visual-origin values, two choices, and five 4×4 matrices.

Read the joint graph

Use joint transforms for kinematics

\[{}^{P}T_C(q_i)=T(x,y,z)R_z(\mathrm{yaw})R_y(\mathrm{pitch})R_x(\mathrm{roll})R_{\hat a_i}(q_i)\]

Inspect each revolute joint

Read its parent, child, origin and axis. The origin is expressed in the parent frame; the axis is expressed in the joint frame.

URDF link frames do not have to be D–H frames. Locate the joint axes before choosing the D–H frames.

Mesh appearance comes from <visual><origin>. These offsets do not enter the joint-to-tool forward kinematics.
