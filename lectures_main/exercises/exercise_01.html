Part 2 · Product of exponentials

Derive one world-frame screw for each joint

Follow Lecture 02: use the space form and angular-first coordinates. Every screw is expressed in world at the supplied home configuration \(q=\mathbf0\).

\[{}^{W}T_{J_i}(0)=\begin{bmatrix}R_i&\mathbf r_i\\\mathbf0^T&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad \boldsymbol\omega_i=R_i\frac{\mathbf a_i}{\|\mathbf a_i\|},\qquad \boldsymbol\xi_i=\begin{bmatrix}\boldsymbol\omega_i\\-\boldsymbol\omega_i\times\mathbf r_i\end{bmatrix}\]

Use the declared positive axis

All seven joints are revolute. Each \(\boldsymbol\omega_i\) is a unit direction; \(\mathbf v_i=-\boldsymbol\omega_i\times\mathbf r_i\). The vector \(\mathbf r_i\) points from the world origin to a point on joint \(i\)’s axis.

Keep the same joint signs and zeros as the simulator.

Store the home tool pose

Compute \(M={}^{world}T_{ee}(\mathbf0)\), including every fixed edge. Fixed joints contribute to the geometry and to \(M\); they do not add screw coordinates.

Keep your axis points and derivation with your FK code.

Part 3 · Implement forward kinematics

Build PoE and D–H FK from your parameters

PoE implementation

Write fk_poe(q) using your seven space screws and \(M\).

\[T_{\mathrm{PoE}}(q)=\left(\prod_{i=1}^{7}e^{\widehat{\xi}_i q_i}\right)M\]

Use \(\widehat\xi_i=\begin{bmatrix}[\omega_i]_\times&v_i\\0&0\end{bmatrix}\). Evaluate the exponential with a matrix-exponential routine or the closed form from Lecture 02.

D–H implementation

Write fk_dh(q) using your D–H table and fixed transforms.

\[T_{\mathrm{DH}}(q)=T_{base}\left(\prod_{i=1}^{7}A_i(q_i)\right)T_{tool}\]

Use the standard-D–H order from Lecture 01. Include the constant theta offsets, and multiply the joint factors in root-to-tool order.

Both functions take the same seven joint values in radians and return a 4×4 world-to-iiwa_link_ee transform. Keep the two calculations independent and save your runnable code.
