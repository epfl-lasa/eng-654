03 · KUKA iiwa 7 · choose redundancy

Give each IK set two successful starting angles

The circular path and tool orientation are fixed. For each of the eight algebraic IK sets, choose two distinct starting q₃ values from the supplied selection. A choice is correct when the analytical or numerical planner completes the path from that selected start.

These sets label the fixed-q₃ algebraic roots. They are local labels, not eight globally disconnected components. Your chosen q₃ specifies the starting posture; both planners may change q₃ along the path. The analytical planner connects exact IK candidates across redundant angles and sets, refining a failed coarse search. Completing the task-space loop does not require returning to the initial redundant posture.

What do the two planners check?

The analytical planner searches validated connections between exact IK candidates. The numerical planner uses the geometric Jacobian and a null-space joint-limit cost, then corrects the pose. Both preserve native joint limits and check continuous joint motion.
