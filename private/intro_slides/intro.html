The engineering standard we will develop

Global analysis and guarantees

Feasibility over
the entire motion.

With explicit assumptions.
  1. The allowed robot posturesWhere do joint limits, collisions and singularities restrict motion?
  2. The connections between posturesWhich postures can the robot move between continuously?
  3. The evidence for executionWhich constraints hold along the path, and what remains unverified?

A guarantee is relative to the model and constraints. Checking isolated samples alone does not certify the motion between them.

Theme: A guarantee describes what holds along a complete path, within a stated model and set of assumptions.

Scope: Allowed postures, continuous connections between them, and constraints along the route. Checks at isolated points can miss what happens between them.

Context: Physical safety also depends on sensing, control and operating conditions.
