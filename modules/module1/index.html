Click a visible mesh to select it · W = translate · E = rotate · R = scale visual mesh · Q = local/world
URDF semantics: dragging a visual changes that link's <visual><origin>. Dragging a joint changes <joint><origin> and therefore moves the complete downstream chain. The RGB arrows show the joint frame axes (X red, Y green, Z blue), the white dot is the exact frame origin, and the yellow line/arrow shows the URDF rotation axis for revolute/continuous joints. Scaling is written to the selected mesh's scale attribute.
